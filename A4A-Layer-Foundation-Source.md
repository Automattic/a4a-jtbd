# A4A JTBD: Layer Foundation Source Material

_Reference foundation for building the A4A Jobs To Be Done map. Captures the offering (Layer 1 pillars) and the product surface (Layer 2 major functions). No JTBD written yet: this is the raw material to map customer feedback against later._

Jeff's three-layer model (from comment #30385):

1. **Primary pillars**: Earn, Build, Grow (highest-level JTBD)
2. **Major functions**: the dashboard's top-level areas (mid-level JTBD rolling up to pillars)
3. **Micro functions**: granular tasks within each major function (handled last)

## LAYER 1: Primary Pillars (Earn / Build / Grow)

Source: https://automattic.com/for-agencies/ , https://automattic.com/for-agencies/earn/ , plus the #build and #grow sections.

**Program framing / positioning.** "The growth engine for your WordPress agency." The official WordPress partner program; 8,000+ agencies. Tagline: _"Scale, Earn, Stand Out."_ Free to join, no minimums, no exclusivity, no mandatory migrations, no requirement to host with Automattic. Tiers: **Activated → Partner → Pro Partner → Premier Partner**, with benefits unlocking up the ladder (directory listings, badges, lead distribution, assigned partner manager, co-marketing, extension credits, MDF). Loose pillar-level JTBD examples Jeff gave: _"upscale my agency operations," "automate site management," "create another passive revenue stream."_

### EARN: recurring, compounding revenue
The core promise: turn one-off project work into predictable, recurring, compounding income. "Stop chasing projects." Multiple stackable revenue streams across the client lifecycle:

- **20% recurring revenue share on hosting** (WordPress.com / Pressable) on every renewal: via referral link or agency-managed subscription.
- **50% recurring commission on Jetpack plans and WooCommerce extensions.**
- **Bulk/wholesale discounts (up to 80% off) for resale margins**: white-label model; agency controls pricing, margins, billing, and client relationship.
- **WooPayments passive revenue**: 0.05% of total payment volume (TPV) on every transaction a referred store processes, automatically, no account management.
- Commissions earned on **initial purchase and every renewal**.
- Up to **50% recurring commissions** headline; **$3M+** in pre-qualified projects delivered to partners since June 2024.
- Mechanics: track referrals/purchases/revenue in dashboard; commissions paid quarterly via Tipalti. Clients must buy through referral link or pre-built referral cart to qualify.

### BUILD: the agency-first product & hosting stack
"Fewer tools. Faster workflows." Manage all sites, clients, and products from one dashboard. Centralized client + site management (licenses, updates, billing combined). A **build-a-cart referral tool** (custom cart from 60+ Automattic products; clients pay directly). The stack:

- **Hosting**: WordPress.com (fully-managed, agency rates), Pressable (WP Cloud, edge caching, resource isolation), WordPress VIP (enterprise scale/compliance).
- **WooCommerce**: custom scalable stores; key extensions in-program (Subscriptions, Memberships, Bookings, Product Add-ons, Composite Products); payments via WooPayments.
- **Jetpack**: real-time backups, spam protection, malware scanning, WAF, Core Web Vitals optimization, video hosting, site search, stats, SEO, social auto-posting, downtime monitoring.

### GROW: visibility, leads, support, expertise
Tier-gated growth benefits:

- **Partner directory listings** across WooCommerce.com, Pressable.com, Jetpack.com, WordPress.com: visibility to businesses seeking expert help.
- **Warm, qualified lead distribution** (Pro+ partners) from Automattic sales teams: no cold calls.
- **Priority program & product support**: faster response, direct access to technical SMEs; priority migration support.
- **Assigned Partner Manager** (Pro+): strategic support, co-marketing, growth advice.
- **Expert guidance / training**: training sessions, product deep dives, early access to new product launches.
- Sub-pages for deeper detail: `/expert-guidance/` and `/client-management/` (not yet fetched).

## How these layers connect (per Jeff's plan)

- **Layer 1 (pillars)** = durable, high-level jobs an agency hires A4A for. Roughly: Earn ↔ revenue, Build ↔ deliver/operate client sites efficiently, Grow ↔ win more business & level up the agency.
- **Layer 2 (major functions)** = the dashboard areas that get those jobs done; each rolls up to one or more pillars.
- **Layer 3 (micro functions)** = granular tasks inside each function (e.g., add agency logo to a referral, remove a team member's access, modify & resend a client report).

Plan: start with the top 3 pillar JTBD, work down the levels, grounded in incoming customer feedback. Each statement to follow the Session 1 formula: _When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]_: and stay solution-free.
