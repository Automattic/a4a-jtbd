Pillars: the high-level jobs an agency hires A4A for

Earn
Recurring, compounding revenue: turning project work into predictable income that recurs on its own (commissions, revenue share, resale margins).
Build
Deliver and operate client sites efficiently: running many sites reliably from one place on infrastructure the agency trusts.
Grow
Win more of the right business and level up: qualified demand, credibility, and expertise so the agency grows beyond word-of-mouth.
Multi-pillar
A job that meaningfully serves more than one pillar at once (e.g., onboarding spans Earn, Build, and Grow).

Badges: job states & flags

Upcoming
Feature not yet released (also shown with a dashed card + striped header).
New user
Job is part of the first-run / new-user experience.
Returning
Job is for returning / steady-state users.
Intermittent
Availability comes and goes (e.g., a promo/incentive that turns on and off).
Redundant
Duplicates functionality elsewhere: flagged for the consolidation pass. See the job's note.
Tier-gated
Only available to certain agency tiers (e.g., Agency / Pro / Premier Partners).

Layers: the levels of the map, top-down

Pillar (Layer 1)
The highest-level, durable jobs: Earn, Build, Grow. Everything below rolls up to these.
Function (Layer 2)
The dashboard's major functional areas (Sites, Referrals, Migrations, etc.): mid-level jobs that roll up to a pillar.
Micro (Layer 3)
The granular tasks within a function (e.g., add an agency logo to a referral, remove a team member's access).
